From pin to piece

Your Pinterest board, made real.

Send us the piece you have saved. Our workshop in Lahore makes it in your size, your fabric and your finish, and delivers it anywhere in Pakistan.

  1. Send the pin

    Share the Pinterest link or a screenshot on WhatsApp or Instagram, with a photo of the room if you have one.

  2. Drawing and samples

    We return a measured drawing and show you real samples of wood, stone, metal and fabric.

  3. Made by hand

    Carving, gilding, lacquer and upholstery are done by our craftsmen. You see progress photos as it takes shape.

  4. Delivered

    Wrapped, delivered and placed in your home, in Lahore or anywhere in the country.
