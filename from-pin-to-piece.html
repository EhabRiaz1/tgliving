From pin to piece

You saved it. We make it.

TG Living turns the furniture on your Pinterest board into real pieces for your home, built by hand in Lahore.

I · Drawing

Every pin becomes a drawing.

A photograph rarely shows how a piece is built. We study it, ask about your room, and draw it to size with every joint and finish named. Nothing is made until you approve the drawing.

II · Carving

Carved the way it always was.

Our frames are carved by hand, in a tradition you can see in panels like this one from sixteenth-century India. Then they are gilded, lacquered or oiled.

III · Finishing

Brass, velvet and gold leaf.

Metalwork, upholstery and gilding happen under one roof, so the finish you chose from a sample is the finish you receive. We send progress photos along the way.

Questions

Can you make anything I find on Pinterest?

Most furniture, yes. Send it over and we will tell you honestly what we can make, what we would change, and why.

Can I change the size, fabric or finish?

Always. Every piece is drawn for your room, and you choose the wood, stone, metal and fabric from real samples.

How long does it take?

It depends on the piece. We give you a timeline together with the drawing and the quote, before anything is made.

Do you deliver outside Lahore?

Yes. We deliver nationwide and place the piece in your home.
